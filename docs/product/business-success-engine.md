# Business Success Engine

## Purpose

The Business Success Engine is the decision-making framework that powers the Business Success Platform.

Its purpose is to understand each member's business, identify the most valuable opportunities available to them, and recommend the next best actions that will help move their business forward.

Rather than requiring members to search through resources, programs, or services, the engine proactively delivers personalized guidance based on each business's unique context, helping entrepreneurs focus on growth instead of complexity.

## Core Intelligence Capabilities

The Business Success Intelligence (BSI) continuously performs five core capabilities to help members make progress throughout their business journey.

### 1. Understand

The BSI builds a comprehensive understanding of each member and their business by analyzing their profile, business characteristics, goals, current stage, certifications, membership benefits, and historical activity.

The objective is to understand the complete business context before making any recommendation.

---

### 2. Analyze

The BSI evaluates all available opportunities, deadlines, programs, events, services, and business resources to determine which ones are relevant to the member's current situation.

Analysis considers both immediate needs and long-term business growth.

---

### 3. Prioritize

Rather than presenting every available option, the BSI prioritizes the recommendations with the highest potential impact based on urgency, business goals, eligibility, and expected value.

Members should always feel they know what matters most.

---

### 4. Guide

The BSI transforms recommendations into clear, actionable guidance by explaining why each recommendation matters and providing an obvious next step.

Every recommendation should help members move their business forward with confidence.

---

### 5. Learn

The BSI continuously improves its recommendations by learning from member interactions, completed milestones, changing business goals, and platform activity.

As businesses evolve, recommendations should evolve with them.

## Vision

The Business Success Intelligence (BSI) aims to become the trusted decision-making companion for every NYWCC member.

Its vision is to proactively understand each entrepreneur's business, anticipate their needs, and continuously deliver the right guidance, opportunities, and support at the right time.

Rather than acting as a recommendation engine, the BSI should become an intelligent business companion that evolves alongside each entrepreneur throughout their business journey.

## Inputs

The Business Success Intelligence (BSI) uses multiple sources of information to understand each member's context and deliver meaningful recommendations.

Rather than relying on a single data point, the BSI combines information about the member, their business, their goals, and available opportunities to determine the next best actions.

### Member

Information about the individual using the platform.

Examples:
- Member profile
- Preferred language
- Membership type
- Membership status

### Business

Core information about the business.

Examples:
- Business name
- Industry
- Location
- Number of employees
- Years in operation

### Business Stage

The current stage of the business journey.

Examples:
- Startup
- Growing
- Scaling
- Established

### Business Goals

The objectives the entrepreneur wants to achieve.

Examples:
- Increase revenue
- Hire employees
- Win government contracts
- Expand to new markets
- Improve digital presence

### Business Milestones

Important achievements already completed by the business.

Examples:
- Business registered
- Website launched
- First employee hired
- MWBE certified
- First grant received

### Opportunities

Programs and initiatives currently available.

Examples:
- Grants
- Events
- Advisory sessions
- Training programs
- Member benefits

### Activity

The member's interactions with the platform.

Examples:
- Completed recommendations
- Booked consultations
- Registered events
- Downloaded resources

### Time

Time-sensitive information that affects prioritization.

Examples:
- Application deadlines
- Upcoming events
- Membership renewal
- Expiring benefits

## Decision Framework

The Business Success Intelligence (BSI) follows a structured decision framework to determine the most valuable recommendations for each member.

Instead of simply displaying available information, the BSI evaluates the member's current context, identifies opportunities with the greatest potential impact, and prioritizes actions that will help move the business forward.

Every recommendation should answer one question:

**"What is the next best action for this business?"**
