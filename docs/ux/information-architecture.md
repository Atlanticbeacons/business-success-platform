# Information Architecture

## Purpose

The Information Architecture defines how members navigate through the Business Success Platform.

Its purpose is to organize the platform into a simple, intuitive, and scalable structure that helps members quickly find information, complete important tasks, and confidently grow their businesses.

The architecture follows the principle that members should always know where they are, where they can go next, and how every section contributes to their business growth.

## Navigation

The primary navigation provides members with direct access to the core areas of the Business Success Platform.

### Primary Navigation

- Home
- My Businesses
- Funding
- Get Help
- Events
- Benefits
- Resources
- Profile

The Home serves as the primary entry point and personalized business briefing.

All navigation is contextual to the currently selected business.

## Navigation Principles

The navigation of the Business Success Platform is designed to be simple, predictable, and business-centered.

### Business-Centered Navigation

The currently selected business defines the context across the entire platform. All personalized content, recommendations, opportunities, and progress are specific to the active business.

---

### Home as the Starting Point

The Home serves as the primary entry point for members. From the Home, members should be able to access the most important actions and opportunities without unnecessary navigation.

---

### Clear Navigation Paths

Members should always understand:

- Where they are.
- What they can do next.
- How to return to previous screens.

Navigation should never create confusion.

---

### Progressive Disclosure

Present information progressively.

Show only the information needed to make the next decision, allowing members to explore additional details when necessary.

---

### External Services

When an action requires interaction with an external organization (such as a government agency), the platform should clearly indicate that the member is leaving the Business Success Platform.

Whenever possible, the platform should prepare members before redirecting them to the official external website.

---

### Consistency

Navigation patterns, terminology, buttons, and interactions should remain consistent across all screens.

Members should not have to learn different navigation behaviors in different parts of the platform.

## Future Evolution

As the Business Success Platform grows, the information architecture may evolve to support new capabilities while preserving a simple and intuitive experience.

Potential enhancements include:

- Personalized navigation based on member behavior.
- Quick Actions accessible from any screen.
- AI-powered global search.
- Recently viewed opportunities and resources.
- Favorite pages and saved items.
- Personalized shortcuts based on business goals.
- Advisor-specific navigation.
- Administrative navigation for NYWCC staff.
