# Resource Details

## Goal

The Resource Details screen helps members understand and use a specific resource that can support their business goals, address a current need, or help them take a recommended action.

Its purpose is to turn educational content and business tools into practical, actionable value.

---

## User Questions

When members access this screen, they should be able to answer the following questions:

* What is this resource?
* Why is it relevant to my business?
* What will I learn or accomplish?
* How can I use it?
* How much time will it take?
* Where did this resource come from?
* What should I do next?

---

## Success Criteria

A successful Resource Details experience enables members to:

* Quickly understand the purpose of the resource.
* Determine whether it is relevant to their business.
* Access or use the resource easily.
* Understand how the resource supports their goals.
* Take a meaningful next step after using the resource.

---

## Sections

### 1. Resource Overview

Provides a concise summary of the resource.

Information includes:

* Resource Name
* Resource Type
* Topic
* Estimated Time
* Source
* Last Updated

Purpose:

* Help members understand the resource at a glance.

---

### 2. Why This Resource Is Recommended

Explains why the resource is relevant to the member.

Examples:

* Supports your current business goal.
* Helps address an area where your business needs improvement.
* Supports one of your Recommended Actions.
* Helps improve your Business Readiness.

Purpose:

* Make personalized recommendations transparent and useful.

---

### 3. What You'll Learn or Accomplish

Explains the practical value of the resource.

Information may include:

* Key Topics
* Learning Outcomes
* Expected Business Outcome
* Skills or Knowledge Developed

Purpose:

* Help members understand the value before investing time.

---

### 4. Resource Content

Provides access to the actual resource.

Depending on the resource type, this may include:

* Article
* Guide
* Video
* Template
* Checklist
* Toolkit
* Downloadable Document
* External Website

Primary Action:

* Access Resource

---

### 5. Next Steps

Suggests practical actions members can take after using the resource.

Examples:

* Complete a Recommended Action.
* Book a consultation.
* Explore a related funding opportunity.
* Review another resource.
* Update Business Details.

Purpose:

* Turn learning into business progress.

---

### 6. Related Resources

Displays other resources that may be useful based on the member's business context or the current resource.

Primary Actions:

* View Related Resource
* Explore Resource Library

---

## Primary Actions

Members should be able to:

* Access the resource.
* Save the resource for later.
* Remove the resource from Saved Resources.
* Share the resource.
* Download the resource when applicable.
* Visit the official source when the resource is external.
* Take a recommended next step.

---

## Business Rules

### Content

* Every resource must have a clear title, type, topic, and source.
* Resources should display a last-updated date when available.
* Outdated or unavailable resources must not be presented as current.
* External resources should link to the official source whenever possible.

### Recommendations

* The reason for recommending a resource should be displayed when personalized.
* Recommendations should consider the member's active business, goals, readiness, and current context.
* Resources that directly support a Recommended Action should be prioritized.

### External Resources

* The platform must clearly identify resources hosted by external organizations.
* Members should be informed when they are leaving the Business Success Platform.
* External links should point to the official source.

### Saved Resources

* Members can save resources for future reference.
* Saved resources should remain accessible from the Resources screen.
* If a saved resource becomes unavailable, the member should be informed.

### Next Steps

* Suggested next steps should be relevant to the resource and member context.
* The platform should avoid presenting excessive follow-up actions.
* Recommended next steps should prioritize meaningful progress over additional content consumption.

---

## Empty States

### Resource Unavailable

Message:

"This resource is no longer available."

Primary Action:

Explore Related Resources

---

### Resource Content Unavailable

Message:

"We can't access this resource right now."

Primary Action:

Return to Resources

---

### Related Resources Unavailable

Message:

"We don't have any related resources to recommend right now."

Primary Action:

Explore Resource Library
