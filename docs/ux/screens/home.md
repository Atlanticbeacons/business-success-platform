# Home

## Goal

Help members quickly understand the current state of their business and identify the most valuable next actions to continue growing.

---

## User Questions

When a member lands on this screen, they should immediately find answers to:

- What should I do next?
- Are there any important deadlines?
- What opportunities are available for me?
- What events should I attend?
- What benefits can I use today?

---

## Success Criteria

A member should understand what actions to take within the first 30 seconds without having to navigate to another page.
