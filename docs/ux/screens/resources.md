## Sections

The Resources screen is organized to help members discover practical information and tools based on their business needs, goals, and current stage of growth.

### 1. Recommended Resources

Displays resources that are most relevant to the member's active business and current needs.

Purpose:

* Surface useful information without requiring members to search for it.
* Help members learn or take action on topics relevant to their business.

Information includes:

* Resource Name
* Resource Type
* Why it is recommended
* Relevant Business Goal or Need

Primary Action:

* View Resource

---

### 2. Resource Library

Provides access to the complete collection of resources available to members.

Purpose:

* Allow members to explore resources beyond personalized recommendations.

Resource types may include:

* Guides
* Articles
* Templates
* Checklists
* Videos
* Webinars
* Toolkits
* Educational Materials

Primary Actions:

* Search
* Filter
* Sort
* View Resource

---

### 3. Topics

Allows members to explore resources by business topic.

Purpose:

* Make it easier to find information when members know the area they need help with.

Example topics:

* Funding
* Marketing
* Finance
* Legal
* Hiring
* Operations
* Government Contracting
* Certifications
* Business Strategy
* Technology

Primary Action:

* Explore Topic

---

### 4. Saved Resources

Displays resources the member has saved for future reference.

Purpose:

* Allow members to return to useful information without searching again.

Information includes:

* Resource Name
* Resource Type
* Date Saved

Primary Actions:

* View Resource
* Remove from Saved

---

## Primary Actions

Members should be able to:

* View a resource.
* Search the resource library.
* Filter resources by topic or type.
* Sort resources.
* Explore resources by topic.
* Save a resource for later.
* Remove a saved resource.
* Access external resources through the official source when applicable.

---

## Business Rules

The Resources experience must prioritize relevance, clarity, and practical value.

### Recommendations

* Recommended Resources should be based on the member's active business, goals, readiness, and current context.
* Every recommended resource should explain why it is relevant when appropriate.
* Resources that directly support a Recommended Action should be prioritized.

### Content

* Resources must have a clearly defined title, type, and topic.
* Outdated or unavailable resources must not be presented as current.
* Resources should clearly identify their source when content comes from an external organization.
* External resources should link to the official source whenever possible.

### Search and Discovery

* Members should be able to search across the resource library.
* Members should be able to filter by topic and resource type.
* Search and filtering should not hide recommended resources unless the member explicitly applies a filter.

### Saved Resources

* Members can save resources for future reference.
* Saved resources should remain accessible from the Saved Resources section.
* If a saved resource becomes unavailable, the member should be informed.

### Personalization

* Changing the active business should update recommended resources when business context affects relevance.
* Resource recommendations should support the member's current goals and recommended actions.

## Empty States

### No Recommended Resources

Message:

"We don't have any resources specifically recommended for your business right now."

Primary Action:

Explore the Resource Library

---

### No Search Results

Message:

"We couldn't find resources matching your search or filters."

Primary Action:

Clear Filters

---

### No Saved Resources

Message:

"You haven't saved any resources yet."

Primary Action:

Explore Recommended Resources

---

### No Resources for Topic

Message:

"There are no resources available for this topic right now."

Primary Action:

Explore Other Topics
